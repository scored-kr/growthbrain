# Growth Brain startup context

Updated 2026-11-05. Sources: repository implementation, the founder's chat reports, and the uploaded BizForge business plan (document claims are distinguished below).
This is review context, not a claim of verified market traction.

- Product: local encrypted memory shared through product MCP; common preferences then explicitly selected/uniquely triggered project decisions; candidates require user approval.
- Current stage: implemented technical prototype. Actual fresh AI session → MCP context call → personalized output remains blocked/unverified. Public launch and company funding stage: TBD.
- Customer evidence: founder reports **4 real interviews**, with some intention to use. Positive respondent count, interview questions, intensity, actual usage and payment: TBD; do not infer 4/4.
- Synthetic evidence: 70 of 100 metadata-generated fictional customers expressed interest. This informs hypotheses only; it is not observed demand, conversion, retention, a representative survey or a market-size estimate.
- Broad audience named by founder: business owners, freelancers and marketers using AI heavily for projects. Target is explicitly undecided.
- Initial segment to TEST: freelance/small-team marketers juggling several client projects, repeating brand/budget/channel decisions across AI sessions. Client/tool usage, pain frequency, switching and willingness to pay remain unverified.
- Founder priority (2026-11-04): **context handoff** is central. Resume the purpose, decisions and reasons, completed work, unresolved blockers and next action across sessions/AI clients. Preference recall alone is insufficient.
- Positioning hypothesis: resume client work with approved latest context, without manually locating and copying past decisions. Cross-AI continuity, provenance and device-local protection are proposed reasons to choose it.
- Alternatives: native AI project/memory features, project instructions, reusable briefs and manual workflows. MCP/encryption/indexing alone do not establish a moat.
- Business model/pricing: the 2026-11-05 PDF proposes monthly KRW14,900 or annual KRW142,800 (KRW11,900/month equivalent), a 7-day trial and later team/add-on plans. These are document hypotheses, not approved live prices or observed willingness to pay. No known actual MRR, ARR, CAC, retention, churn or LTV. Support time is a material cost hypothesis, even with local compute.
- Team, founder background, runway, financing history/goal, market size: TBD. Do not fill these with zero or invented numbers.
- Current goal: complete one supported client's real MCP personalization flow; validate a narrow pilot's benefit and setup burden; repair proven privacy lifecycle defects.
- Constraints: plaintext and indexes on the user's device; server ciphertext only; equal memory protection across free/paid; explicit consent and approval; no project-to-global promotion; no secrets in AI/MCP/logs/Git. Cloud executor is not the user's device.
- Not authorized by this context: production deployment, customer outreach, fundraising submissions, opening real customer vaults or resetting existing server data.
